# EMOJI ELEMENTS: Spell and Artifact Effects

What every instant and artifact in `CARD_DATABASE` actually does in the live game (`index.js`). It is checked
by `tests/effects.test.mjs` (`npm run effects`). Turn rules are in [RULES.md](RULES.md); combat and keywords
are in RULES.md §8. Card details now show a **Rules:** line for every instant and artifact, generated by
`describeEffect()`, so the in-game text and this page say the same thing.

**Totals:** 70 effect cards: 41 instants and 29 artifacts, using 17 effects (25 rows below, counting variants).

## 1. How effects resolve

- **E1 Timing.** You play instants and artifacts during your main phase, and the AI during its own (RULES.md
  R3.3). Each card resolves when it is played. Then dead creatures are removed (state-based actions), and a
  match that reached 0 life ends at once.
  - Instants go to the graveyard.
  - Artifacts come in three kinds:
    - **On play** (equipment, Bomb, War Horn, DNA Sequence…): resolve once and are then used up, going to the
      graveyard.
    - **Upkeep** (draw, heal, damage): stay on the battlefield and act at the start of each of their owner's
      turns.
    - **Mana**: stay on the battlefield; tap once per turn for colourless mana.
  - The player (`playCard`) and the AI (`aiPlayCard`) use the same functions: `castInstant` and
    `resolveArtifactOnPlay`.
- **E2 Targets (house rule).** There is no targeting UI. Where a card says "target", the game decides:
  - **Damage, drain and discard** go to the opponent.
  - **Creature effects** choose at random among the legal creatures:
    - destroy, tap and bounce choose **enemy creatures without Hexproof**;
    - buffs choose **your own creatures**, Hexproof included.
  - **Sweepers** (Earthquake, Avalanche, Bomb) choose nothing, so they hit Hexproof creatures.
  - With no legal creature, the card does nothing and the log says so. The card is still used up.
- **E3 Leaving the battlefield (house rule).** A creature that dies, is destroyed or is bounced goes back to
  its **printed card**. Stat bonuses, granted keywords, damage, Anger and Deep Freeze all end. A revived or
  replayed creature starts fresh. Tokens keep their token stats.
  - **Changed:** buffs and granted keywords used to stay on the card in the graveyard and in hand, so a bounced
    or revived creature came back still pumped.
- **E4 Duration.**
  - Stat bonuses and granted keywords last while the creature stays on the battlefield, except "until end of
    turn" (Anger), which ends when that turn ends.
  - Damage wears off between turns (C11).
  - A Deep Frozen creature skips its controller's next untap.
  - A granted keyword is never listed twice, but stat bonuses stack (two Feathers make +2/+2 with one Flying).
- **E5 Upkeep.**
  - Each upkeep artifact acts once at the start of its owner's turn, never on the opponent's turn. Two copies
    act twice.
  - An artifact that is no longer on the battlefield stops at once.
  - Upkeep effects can win or lose the match (drawing from an empty deck too, R6.1). After that nothing else
    happens.

## 3. Mismatches found and how they were settled

| Card(s) | Card text said | Engine did | Choice | Changed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Earthquake, Avalanche | "Deal 2/3 to all creatures" | Hit only enemy creatures | Follow the text: both sides (the code comment said "Damage all creatures" too). The AI now holds them when they'd hurt it more. | Code |
| Anger | "+3/+0 … until end of turn" | Permanent +3/+0 | Follow the text | Code |
| Deep Freeze | "Tap target creature, it doesn't untap" | Same as Freeze (+1 cost) | Follow the text: skips its next untap | Code |
| Thought Steal | "Enemy discards 1, you draw 1" | No draw if the enemy hand was empty | Follow the text: always draw | Code |
| Drain Life | "Deal 2, gain 2 life" | A lethal drain skipped the life gain | Gain first, then deal | Code |
| Buffed creatures leaving play | — | Kept bonuses in hand/graveyard | Printed card again (E3) | Code |
| Mana artifacts on the AI side | "Generate extra mana" | The AI never used them | The AI taps them like lands | Code |
| Fireball, Explosion, Inferno, Comet Strike | "Deal N damage to target" | Always hit the opponent | Keep (no targeting UI): "Deal N damage to the enemy" | Text |
| Wrestle | "Destroy target creature in combat" | Destroys any time (instants are main-phase only) | Keep: "Destroy target creature" | Text |
| Magic Wand | "Deal 2 damage when activated" | 2 damage every upkeep, no activation | Keep: "Deal 2 damage to the enemy each turn" (balance is open, §4) | Text |
| Bomb | "Deal 2 to all enemies" | Each enemy creature only | Keep: "Deal 2 to each enemy creature" | Text |
| War Horn, Protective Amulet, Footsteps | "All creatures …" | Only your creatures | Keep: "Your creatures …" | Text |
| Dark Scroll | "Draw extra card" | Every upkeep | Keep: "Draw an extra card each turn" | Text |
| Equipment (Flaming Sword, Battle Axe, …) | "Equipped creature gets …" | One random creature, permanently; the artifact is used up | Keep as a house rule. The Rules line says so; the card text is unchanged. | Rules line |
| "target creature" on destroy/tap/buff cards | "target" | Random legal creature | Keep as a house rule (E2). The Rules line says "random". | Rules line |

None of these text changes affected rarity, pack themes or deck legality (checked by regenerating the card
manifest, which also lists each card's Rules line).

## 4. Open choices (not changed)

| Topic | Now | Why it's open |
|---|---|---|
| Real targeting | Random targets (E2) | Needs targeting UI for the player and target choice for the AI. |
| Magic Wand | 2 damage every upkeep for 2 mana; Cursed Orb does 1 for 3 | Probably over-tuned. It could become a tap ability or deal 1. |
| Equipment | One-shot, permanent bonus | True equipment (stays on the battlefield, moves when its creature dies) would need new rules and UI. |
| Wasted casts | You can cast a buff with no creature, or a tap spell with no legal target; it is used up | It could be refused instead, like an unaffordable card. |
| Bounced tokens | Go to hand as a free 1/1 or 2/2 card | In many card games, tokens stop existing when they leave the battlefield. |
| Flash | No effect (RULES.md C15) | Needs an instant-speed window. |
