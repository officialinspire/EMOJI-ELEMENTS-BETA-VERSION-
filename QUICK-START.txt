🚀 TO PLAY:
   1. Serve the folder (python -m http.server 8000) and open
      http://localhost:8000 - or open index.html directly
   2. Tap CLICK TO START (this also turns on sound), then tap to
      skip the intro or let it play

🎯 WHAT'S IN IT:
   ✅ 268 cards: lands, creatures, instants and artifacts
   ✅ 15 ready decks: 5 single-colour, 10 two-colour
   ✅ 3 AI difficulty levels, all playing by your rules
   ✅ Pack Shop, free pack on every win, Collection, Deck Builder
   ✅ Phone, tablet and desktop; touch, mouse and keyboard
   ✅ Progress saved in your browser (damaged saves are repaired
      and backed up)

🎮 CONTROLS:
   • Tap / click a card to play it, a land stack to tap a land,
     an artifact to use it, a creature (after ATTACK) to attack
   • Long-press / right-click a card for its details
   • Tap a tapped land again to undo it while its mana is unspent
   • Keyboard: Tab to a card + Enter to play, I for details,
     Space/E end turn, A attack, M mulligan, Esc pause/close

📖 GAME RULES:
   • Start with 20 life, reduce opponent to 0
   • Draw 7 cards; mulligan to 6 once, before your first action
   • Play 1 land per turn
   • Tap lands for mana to play cards
   • Attack with creatures
   • Blocking is automatic for both sides
   • Attackers stay tapped until your next turn (unless Vigilance)
   • Drawing from an empty deck loses
   • These are house rules inspired by, not identical to, other
     card games. Full rules: RULES.md

💳 PROGRESSION:
   • New profile: 100 credits, 5 starter decks' worth of cards, 15 ready decks
   • Win: +25 credits and a free booster pack. Loss: +10 credits
   • Pack Shop: 60 credits a pack (Lands pack 40)
   • Decks: exactly 60 cards, max 2 copies (1 legendary); basic lands
     are limited only by the land caps (20 mono, 11 per colour dual)
   • Full rules: PROGRESSION.md

🔧 TECHNICAL:
   • Vanilla JavaScript, no build step, no runtime dependencies
   • localStorage for progress
   • Sound starts after the first tap (browser autoplay rules);
     mute is remembered; "reduce motion" turns off animations
   • Tests: see README.md › Testing
