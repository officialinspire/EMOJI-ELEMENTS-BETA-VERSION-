# Card Catalog Audit

Audit of every card in `CARD_DATABASE` (`index.js`) and every path that makes cards available. It was taken on
branch `claude/compassionate-albattani-aabox6`, on top of the [QA baseline](QA_BASELINE.md).

The machine-readable manifest is **[`docs/card-manifest.json`](docs/card-manifest.json)**. It lists one entry per
card: id, source key, stats, effect, inferred rarity and pack theme, deck-builder copy limit, and availability
(starter copies, starter decks, pack pools, AI decks, the decks where it is legal).

**Verdict:** the data itself is clean. All 268 ids are unique, there are no malformed costs or stats, no unknown
fields, no unsupported effect strings, and no card is unobtainable. This pass fixed three real defects that made
cards unusable (§4). What remains are content and design choices that need your call (§5). The biggest is that
the **Alien and Robot packs contain no alien or robot cards**.

## 1. How it is checked

```bash
node tests/card-audit.mjs           # validate (exit 1 on regression); also: npm test
node tests/card-audit.mjs --write   # after an intended catalog change: refresh the manifest + counts below
```

The script extracts the catalog **and the game's own functions** from `index.js` and runs them in a Node VM, with
no browser and no copied logic. The functions cover starter decks, `validateDeckBuild`, `isCardEligibleForDeck`,
`getThemePool`, `generateDeck` (with a seeded RNG) and `validateMeta`. The "supported" effects and abilities are
read from the engine code (`resolveSpell`, `processUpkeepEffects`, `activateArtifact`, `playCard`, `hasAbility`),
so implementing `flash` updates the check automatically.

It **fails** on any of the following:
- duplicate ids, names or object keys (a JavaScript object silently drops a duplicate key);
- unknown fields (typos), a bad `type` or `cardType`, or an unknown theme;
- a missing name, emoji or description;
- a bad cost colour or amount, or more than 2 colours;
- a bad power or toughness;
- an effect, ability, token type, buff type or target the engine doesn't handle, or a missing or invalid `value`;
- a card that is unobtainable or not legal in any deck;
- a starter deck that is the wrong size, invalid, uses an unowned card, or shrinks on reload;
- a stale manifest or counts block;
- a **removed card id** (saved collections would silently lose it, because `validateMeta` drops unknown ids).

Issues waiting on a decision are listed one by one in `KNOWN_ISSUES` in the script. Any new issue fails, and an
allow-listed issue that disappears also fails, so the list stays exact.

Each failure mode was tested by injecting it into a temporary copy of `index.js`: a renamed card, a field typo
(`toughnes`), an unknown effect (`lifesteal`), a bad cost colour (`fyre`), a duplicate object key, and an unknown
ability (`regenerate`). All six exit 1 with a specific message.

## 3. Availability paths

| Path | Code | What it actually makes available |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter decks** | `buildStarterDeckFromColors`, `createInitialMeta` | 15 decks (5 mono, 10 dual), each 60 cards. Mono decks hold 20 lands, 21 creatures, 9 instants and 10 artifacts. Dual decks hold 22 lands (11 per colour), 20 creatures, 9 instants and 9 artifacts. They are built deterministically from the cheapest eligible cards. The 3 free artifacts appear in **all 15**. |
| **Owned collection** | `createInitialMeta` | Seeded **only from the 5 mono starters**: 153 unique cards, 300 copies, no legendaries. Dual starters are built only from these owned cards, so every starter is legal on a fresh profile (decision G, resolved). |
| **Booster packs** (shop and win) | `getThemePool`, `generatePack` | 5 cards per pack, no duplicates within a pack. Every card is in at least one pool, and every card in a pool can be drawn. 115 cards can only be obtained from packs. See the pool table above: Alien and Robot are 100% neutral filler (decision A). |
| **Deck Builder** | `getDeckBuilderDeckEntries`, `validateDeckBuild` | Only owned cards that are legal for the deck's colours. **267 of 268** cards are legal in at least one standard deck; the exception is Crystal Wasteland (decision H). |
| **Binder** | `renderBinderCollection` | Shows every owned id that exists in the catalog, sorted by name. Duplicate names display as identical rows (decision D). |
| **AI decks** | `generateDeck` | Random decks from the cards whose cost colours are within the AI's colours. **13 cards never appear** in AI decks: the 3 special lands and the 10 colourless-cost artifacts. Separately, QA defect D3 (the AI can be dealt a "colorless" colour) is unchanged. |
| **Saved games** | `validateMeta` | Unknown ids are dropped on load, and deck lists are clamped to owned copies. Ids are `${category}-${slug(name)}`, so **renaming a card deletes it from saves**. The check now blocks that. A fresh save survives a reload unchanged, for all 15 decks. Damaged or older saves are repaired, not replaced ([PROGRESSION.md](PROGRESSION.md) §7). |
| **Tokens** | `resolveSpell` `token` | Spirit, Soldier and Beast tokens exist only during a match. They are not catalog cards and can't be collected. |

## 4. Fixed in this pass

| Fix | Why | Effect on players |
|---|---|---|
| Added descriptions to the 5 basic lands ("Tap for Fire mana", …) | They were the only cards with no rules text in the card detail and binder. | Text only. Rarity and pack theme don't read this text, which was verified. |
| Basic lands are exempt from the Deck Builder 2-copy limit (`getDeckBuilderCopyLimit`) | Starter decks hold 20–22 basic lands, so **every** deck failed validation and nothing could be saved (QA defect D2). That blocked every pack card from ever reaching a deck. Land totals are still capped by `getDeckLandCaps` (20 mono, 11 per colour dual). | Starter and edited decks can be saved. |
| Land deck legality follows the colours a land produces (`isCardEligibleForDeck`), the same rule `validateDeckBuild` already applied on save | **Rainbow** ("Tap for any color mana") was legal in **no** deck, and Distant Planet only in SWAMP_LIGHT. | Rainbow is legal in all 15 decks and Distant Planet in 9. Crystal Wasteland is no longer offered in the pool only to fail on save. |

**Not changed:** pack odds, pools, rarities, pack themes, starter contents, AI pools, prices, and the collections
of existing players. Manifests generated before and after the fixes were diffed: rarity, pack theme, pool
membership, pool sizes, starter contents and AI availability are identical for all 268 cards. No cards are
granted to anyone.

## 5. Decisions needed

Each item is listed in `KNOWN_ISSUES` (or noted as text-only) until you decide.

| # | Issue | Options (recommendation first) |
|---|---|---|
| A | **Pack themes don't match their cards.** `inferTheme` maps every `Science Fiction` card to `scifi` before checking keywords. As a result the **Alien and Robot pools contain 0 matching cards** (111 Nature/City "neutral filler" cards each), and Tech has 6 matches + 111 filler. All 14 robot, alien, drone, mech, cyborg and android cards are only in SciFi. Keyword false positives: Badlands Boar is in the Lands pool ("land"); Sky Eagle and Lantern Guard count as Tech ("watch"), as does Mole Engineer ("engine"); Bomb, War Horn and Heavy Armor count as Tech (their cardType "Artifact"). | 1) Add an explicit pack theme per card, so Alien and Robot actually contain aliens and robots. **This changes pack odds.** 2) Re-label the packs to match the real `theme` field (Nature, City, …). 3) Keep as is. |
| B | **Rarity is inferred, not authored.** `inferRarity` never produces `uncommon` (204 common / 41 rare / 14 epic / 9 legendary). The basic land Ancient Grove is "rare" because its name contains "ancient". Legendaries by colour: fire 4, water 3, earth 1, swamp 1, light 0. The SciFi pool has no legendaries. | 1) Author an explicit `rarity` per card (changes pack odds and copy limits where values differ). 2) Only pin basic lands to common. 3) Keep. |
| C | **3 free artifacts** (cost `{}`): DNA Sequence (draw a card), **Fingerprint Scanner (+1 mana every turn for 0)**, Footsteps (all your creatures gain haste). Similar sci-fi artifacts cost 1–3 colourless. All three are in **all 15 starter decks** (2 copies each). | 1) Give them colourless costs. This regenerates every starter for new players; existing saved decks keep their lists. 2) Keep them as deliberate 0-drops. |
| D | **Duplicate names**: "Signal Tower" and "Chrono Watch" each exist as both a creature and an artifact, with the same emoji. | 1) Rename the creature versions, with a save alias so owned copies carry over. Renaming changes ids, and the check blocks a bare rename. 2) Keep. |
| E | **Keyword with no rules effect**: `flash` (9 creatures). `unblockable` (Otter Scout, Surf's grant) was implemented in the combat pass (RULES.md C3). | 1) Implement Flash: it needs an instant-speed window during the AI turn (gameplay work). 2) Replace it with a supported keyword. |
| F | **Rules text that doesn't match the engine** (15 cards, not allow-listed because it's text): Fireball, Explosion, Inferno and Comet Strike say "target" but hit the opponent. Anger says "until end of turn" but buffs are permanent. Deep Freeze says "doesn't untap" but it untaps (so it's Freeze at +1 cost). Earthquake and Avalanche say "all creatures" but only hit enemy creatures. Six "equipped" artifacts (Flaming Sword, Earth Shield, Heavy Armor, Battle Axe, Nano Shield, Mech Claw) are one-shot buffs to a random creature. Magic Wand says "when activated" but deals 2 **every upkeep**. | 1) Rewrite the text to match the engine now (no balance change), and plan the rules work separately. 2) Implement the described behaviour. |
| G | ✅ **Resolved in the progression pass.** ~~FIRE_SWAMP starter used Web Trap, which nobody owns (QA D10).~~ Dual starters are now built only from owned cards: Curse replaces Web Trap. Saved FIRE_SWAMP decks that were never edited are migrated to the new list; edited decks are left alone; no cards are granted. | Option 1 was taken, without excluding owned two-colour cards. |
| H | **Crystal Wasteland** (colourless land) can be pulled from SciFi and Lands packs but is legal in no deck. | 1) Allow colourless lands in any deck (counting toward the total land cap, not toward colour caps). 2) Remove it from the pools. |
| I | **Copy limit for special lands**: basic lands are now unlimited. Rainbow and Distant Planet stay at 2 (rarity rule). | Keep 2 / make all lands unlimited / some other cap. |
| J | **13 cards never appear in AI decks** (3 special lands, 10 colourless-cost artifacts). | Decide alongside the QA D3 fix (the AI colour pool). |
| K | Minor: lifelink on 0-power creatures does nothing (Baby Chick, Med Capsule). Vigilance on a defender (Leviathan Whale) does nothing. | Tune or keep. |
