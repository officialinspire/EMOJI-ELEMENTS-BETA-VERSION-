🎯 Objective

Reduce your opponent's life from 20 to 0 using creatures, spells, and strategy!

🃏 Cards & Mana

  • Lands - Play one per turn to generate mana
  • Creatures - Cost mana to play, can attack & block
  • Spells - One-time effects (damage, heal, etc.). Where a card says "target", the game picks a random legal creature, or your opponent for damage
  • Artifacts - Some act at the start of each of your turns, mana artifacts tap once per turn, and equipment and other one-shot artifacts act once, then are used up
  • Right-click or long-press a card: its Rules line says exactly what it does

⚔️ Combat

  • Tap lands to generate mana (click on them)
  • Play cards from your hand using mana
  • Click "ATTACK" to select attackers
  • Blocking is automatic for both sides: each attacker is blocked by the toughest untapped creature that can legally block it (two for Menace)
  • Creatures deal damage = their power

⏱️ Turn Rules

  • You go first and skip your first draw. Each later turn: draw, untap, then play.
  • Mulligan once (redraw 6), only before you play or tap anything.
  • One land per turn. A tapped land can be untapped only while its mana is unspent.
  • Unused mana is lost at end of turn. Colorless mana can pay any color.
  • New creatures can't attack until your next turn (unless Haste).
  • Attackers stay tapped until your next turn, so they can't block (Vigilance avoids this).
  • If you must draw from an empty deck, you lose.

✨ Abilities

    📱 Controls

    • Tap or click a hand card to play it, a land stack to tap one land, an artifact to use it, and (after ATTACK) a creature to select it. Tapping any other card, or an enemy card, shows its details.
    • Long-press or right-click a card to see its details. On a land stack it taps all its untapped lands.
    • After combat, a message shows who blocked whom. 💔 marks damage taken this turn; a dashed border means the creature can't attack until next turn.
    • Keyboard: Tab to a card, Enter to play or use it, I for details. Space or E ends the turn, A attacks, M mulligans, Esc closes a window or pauses.

    🧩 Collecting Cards & Building Decks

    • Winning matches awards booster packs to keep your progress growing.
    • Opening packs adds cards directly to your collection.
    • Use Deck Builder to manage the cards you own and assemble your playable decks.
    • Standard deck keys are stable: picking FIRE or FIRE/WATER always loads your saved version for that key.
    • Packs include rarity levels and themed pools like fantasy, sci-fi, tech, alien, robot, and lands.
    • Use Collection (earned cards) to review everything you own and track what you still want to pull.

    📘 Deck Builder Guide

    • You can only add cards you OWN, earned from booster packs.
    • Copy limits apply: normal cards have a max duplicate limit, and legendary cards have a stricter cap.
    • Color identity matters: mono decks use one element, while two-color decks can only use cards legal for both selected elements.
    • Deck size must match the exact requirement; this keeps draws fair and consistent and is required before saving.
    • Standard decks auto-load by selection: choosing FIRE or FIRE/WATER in battle setup uses that saved deck every match.
    • Winning matches earns themed booster packs, expanding your collection and unlocking more deck-building options.
